# Limitations

This work does not establish consciousness, sentience, AGI, psychological
realism, human-equivalent memory, or universal superiority over all alternative
state/memory architectures.

It also does not establish that the full current CMI implementation is minimal.
One important simplification result is explicit: the tested fear dimension was
exactly representable by the tiny reducer family across Stage-3 + Stage-6.

Several stages are post-hoc and are labeled accordingly. Stage 7B repaired a
renderer after observing failures. Stages 13–16 progressively attacked CMI with
compact deterministic replacements. Stage 17 v2 is the prospective holdout for
those frozen relationship reducers.

Stage 17 v1 is invalid/incomplete. It generated only hostile CMI reference states
and aborted before any compact baseline was scored.

Model judges are not ground truth. Token counts do not imply a latency or cost
SLO. Stage-18 timing is telemetry only.

Finally, CMI state is the reference in replacement experiments. Agreement with
CMI is not the same thing as independent proof of psychological correctness.
